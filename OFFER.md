## Pain

The internet's advice on "how to make money" is either unfalsifiable (a guru's
income screenshot), survivorship-biased (only the winners post), or too coarse
to act on (a national salary average with no path attached). Nobody can put a
trade, a tech job, and starting a newsletter on the same ranked board and trust
the comparison.

## Cure

One board, two lenses:

- **Start now**: weighted toward reachability, income over barrier over time.
  The best deck to open with today on little capital.
- **Highest ceiling**: weighted toward terminal pay and trajectory. The best
  deck if you can absorb years of ramp.

Every occupation deck traces to BLS OEWS median wage and 10-year growth
projection. Every internet deck carries a brutal, survivorship-adjusted median
and an explicit data-confidence badge rather than a guru's claim. The Pick,
Meta Breaker, and matchup chart surface the board's actual signal instead of
making the reader scan 90 rows. A weekly report (`/report`) tracks what moved
and why, and says "nothing moved" honestly when nothing did.

## Alternative

- **Guru content and course funnels.** Unfalsifiable income claims, selling the
  course rather than the outcome.
- **Self-reported salary sites.** Wide variance, no verification, no path
  attached to the number.
- **A single BLS lookup.** Accurate but not comparative, and does not cover
  internet or ownership paths at all.
- **Doing nothing and guessing.** The default, and the real competitor.
